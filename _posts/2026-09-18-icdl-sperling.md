Our work at the 2026 IEEE International Conference on Development and Learning (ICDL), in Kyoto, Japan, proposes an evaluation protocol inspired by George Sperling’s experiments to assess sensory and perceptual abilities in cognitive architectures.

The work received the **2nd Best Student Paper Award** at ICDL 2026.

Leonardo L. Rossi, Paula P. Costa, Alexandre Simões, Esther Colombini and Ricardo Gudwin. "Sperling-Inspired Evaluation of Sensory and Perceptual Abilities in Cognitive Architectures." In: 2026 IEEE International Conference on Development and Learning (ICDL), Kyoto, Japan, 2026.
